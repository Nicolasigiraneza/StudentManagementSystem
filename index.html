About the System

The Student Management System is a digital platform designed to improve the efficiency of academic administration by centralizing student information and institutional records.

The system provides an organized way to manage student profiles, trainer information, courses, attendance, academic records, and administrative reports.

Main System Features

Student Management

Register, update, view, and manage student information and profiles.

Trainer Management

Organize trainer information and manage academic responsibilities.

Course Management

Manage courses, academic programs, and course information.

Attendance Management

Record, monitor, and manage student attendance information.

Reports and Records

Generate organized reports and access academic information.

Secure Access

Support controlled system access and protection of institutional data.
